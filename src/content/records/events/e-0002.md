The 97 runs a loop round Palus Magna every twelve minutes. At 17:40 on 9 May the recorded announcement said "Next stop, Marsh Market" between Block 210 and the community centre. The two stops are three hundred metres apart, and there has never been anything between them except a drain.

Someone rang the bell, so the driver stopped. Nine passengers got off. He says the doors opened onto a covered market with wet floors and fish on ice, and that it smelt like rain. He closed the doors and drove on. The next stop was the community centre, as usual. [[note 2-4: Marsh Market was pulled down in 1986. The blocks were built on top of it.]]

All nine passengers were traced afterwards. Eight walked home that evening. [[-5: The ninth, a woman with a shopping trolley, was seen boarding the 97 at the same stop the following Tuesday.]] [[+6: The ninth got home the following Tuesday and could not say where she had been. She had bought fish.]]

The Transit Board has cut the announcement from the bus's tape. On the 9th of every month the tape plays it anyway. ||Nobody has asked the driver to change routes.||
