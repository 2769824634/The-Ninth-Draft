The 97 runs a loop round Toa Payoh every twelve minutes. At 17:40 on Sunday 9 May the recorded announcement said "Next stop, Marsh Market" between Block 210 and the community centre. The two stops are three hundred metres apart, and there has never been anything between them except a drain.

Someone rang the bell, so the driver stopped. Nine passengers got off. He says the doors opened onto a covered market with wet floors and fish on ice, and that it smelt like rain. [[-3: A radio was playing an old Hokkien song at one of the stalls, and a woman at the vegetable stall was calling out prices. Two of his passengers waved to somebody inside.]] He closed the doors and drove on. The next stop was the community centre, as usual. [[+3: The Transit Board took the announcement tape out of the bus the next morning. Between Block 210 and the community centre it has one stop more than the route card.]] [[note 3: Marsh Market was pulled down in 1986. The blocks were built on top of it.]]

The driver did not see any of the nine again that shift. [[+3: All nine were traced afterwards. Eight had walked home that evening and noticed nothing, except that their clothes smelt of fish. The ninth, a woman with a shopping trolley, was reported missing by her family on the Monday. She came home on the Tuesday and could not say where she had been. She had bought fish.]]

He finished his shift and handed in his log at the depot. [[-8: At the bottom of the log he asked to be moved to another route.]] [[+3: The Transit Board has since cut the announcement out of the bus's tape.]] [[+8: On the 9th of every month the tape plays it anyway.]] ||He is still driving the 97.||
