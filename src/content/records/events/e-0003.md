At 21:09 on 9 August the lights went out across Silva. The substation found nothing wrong. At 21:20 the power came back on its own.

When the district office restarted its computers, all thirty-four of them showed the date as 9 August 1900. The clerks set them right by hand, one machine at a time. It took them until midnight. [[note 5-7: Not a fault. This is the year-field problem, exactly. Approve R-0001.]]

Silva's clocks have run an hour ahead of the Axis for as long as anyone can remember, so the outage was logged twice: 21:09 local and 20:09 Axis. [[-6: For those eleven minutes, the street lamps still lit were the ones the district took down in 1952.]] [[#3-8: The substation engineer asked for a transfer the next morning.]]

The Records Office closed the inquiry with a note that the year 1900 "did not persist". Heuss reopened it the same week. It has been under review ever since.
