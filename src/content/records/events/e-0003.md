At 21:09 on 9 August the lights went out across Woodlands. The substation found nothing wrong. At 21:20 the power came back on its own. [[-5: The engineer on duty wrote that no breaker had tripped and that the meters had kept turning the whole time, as if the district had gone on using power in the dark.]]

When the district office restarted its computers, all thirty-four of them showed the date as 9 August 1900. The clerks set them right by hand, one machine at a time. It took them until midnight. [[+5: The Data Section collected the machines' logs the next morning. Anything typed into a machine before it was set right is dated 1900.]] [[note 5-7: Not a fault. This is the year-field problem, exactly. Approve R-0001.]]

Woodlands' clocks have run an hour ahead of the Axis for as long as anyone can remember, so the outage was logged twice: 21:09 local and 20:09 Axis. [[-5: For those eleven minutes, the only street lamps still lit in the district were the ones it took down in 1952.]] [[#5: The engineer on duty asked for a transfer the next morning.]]

The substation wired the Records Office to ask what to do. [[+5: The Data Section classified the file and closed the inquiry with a note that the year 1900 "did not persist".]] [[+7: Heuss reopened it in September and attached the clerks' logs from that night. One entry, typed at 22:40, is a resident's complaint that the lift at Block 573 has broken down again. That lift did not break down until 2 September. The file has been under review ever since.]]
