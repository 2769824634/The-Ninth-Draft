Block 7 in Serrangon has twelve flats on each floor. Every floor but the third is numbered 01 to 12. On the third floor the numbers run from 7-301 to 7-311, and the next door is 7-313.

The residents' committee says this is recent. They remember a 7-312 at the end of the corridor: a bicycle chained to the gate, a birdcage in the window. Nobody can remember the family's name. [[-4: Two neighbours still have the family's spare key.]] [[note 3-4: Keys aren't evidence. Doors are.]]

The Housing Board inspected on 23 November. The corridor is exactly long enough for twelve flats and there is no gap in the wall. The inspector counted the doors twice and the bell buttons in the lift lobby once. ||There are thirteen buttons.||

The case was closed as a numbering mistake from when the door plates were replaced in 1997. In December a parcel addressed to 7-312 was delivered. Somebody signed for it.
