At about two in the morning on 9 March, the driver of the night bus on Route 12 reported that the signs on Bridge Road were wrong. All fourteen of them read Old Dyke Lane, the name the road had before the north bank was filled in in 1981.

The district office sent one officer. By the time he arrived at 06:40 the signs read Bridge Road again. He wrote that the paint was dry and that ||the old name still showed if he held his torch at an angle||. [[-5: Two households said their letters that morning were addressed to Old Dyke Lane.]] [[note 4-5: Letters are the Postal Board's business. Not our file.]]

Frank was still inspecting the north-bank bridge at the time. He measured it that same morning. It was two centimetres longer. He did not mention the signs. [[+6: The Office notes that it did not rain that night.]]

The cause was recorded as a printing fault at the sign workshop. The workshop has no record of printing any signs that month. [[+7: See R-0002, Island Signage Standardisation.]]
