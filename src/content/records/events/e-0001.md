The signal was first logged at 02:14 local time [[#3-6: by Signals Engineer A. Reiss]].
[[-6: Reiss described it as "a voice, not a carrier."]] [[+7: Reiss described it as an unidentified carrier.]]

It repeated every ||eleven minutes and nine seconds|| until 14 December, when it stopped mid-cycle.
[[-4: The array was pointed at the zenith, not the horizon.]] [[note 3-5: Remove the zenith line. Nobody needs to know where it came from.]]
