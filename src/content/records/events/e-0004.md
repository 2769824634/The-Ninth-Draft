Just after six on the evening of 9 October, water started coming up between the paving stones of Hougang. Not a puddle: a line, one thin strip from the ferry steps, past the bakery on Harbour Row, to the back of Block 12.

It stood ankle-deep for forty minutes and then drained away, leaving salt on the pavement and a few strands of seaweed. A Survey Section officer laid the line over the old charts that night. ||It is the shoreline from before the reclamation, accurate to about a metre.||

The Office's public notice blamed a burst water main. [[-4: No main runs under Harbour Row.]] [[note 2-5: The old coast is on the survey map as a red dashed line. Keep the visitors from noticing it.]] [[+6: This file was sealed on 9 November.]]

On the 9th of every month the bakery still sweeps sand out from under its mats.
