Just after six on the evening of 9 October, water started coming up between the paving stones of Hougang. Not a puddle: a line, one thin strip from the ferry steps, past the bakery on Harbour Row, to the back of Block 12. [[-4: The residents' committee says the water was warm, and that children from Block 12 caught two small crabs in it and kept them in a biscuit tin.]]

It stood ankle-deep for forty minutes and then drained away, leaving salt on the pavement and a few strands of seaweed. [[#6: The older residents say the line is where the beach used to be.]] [[+4: On 18 October a Survey Section officer laid the line over the old charts. It follows the coast from before the reclamation to within about a metre.]]

The residents' committee asked for the pavement to be dug up and checked. [[-4: It also asked who had set a mooring ring into the pavement outside the bakery, since it had not been there the week before.]] [[+4: The Water Board found no burst pipe. No main runs under Harbour Row.]] [[note 4: The old coast is on the survey map as a red dashed line. Keep the visitors from noticing it.]] [[+6: The Office's public notice blamed a burst water main. This file was sealed on 9 November.]]

The bakery on Harbour Row opened as usual the next morning. [[+6: On 9 November it swept sand out from under its mats again.]]
