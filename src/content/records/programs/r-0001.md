Every system at the Records Office stores the year in two digits. At midnight on 31 December 1999 they will read 00, and the Office's machines will decide it is 1900. Heuss was given the job of fixing this in January 1998. He was twenty.

His first plan ran to eleven pages: widen the year field to four digits, test it in every district office, switch over on 9 December. The review board sent it back with one line. ||"Nothing wrong with the plan. Cannot be approved."|| He has had the same line back seven more times. [[note 2-7: Ask them what is wrong with it. They'll say nothing. Then ask why.]]

Silva has already shown what happens without the fix: for eleven minutes in August its computers lived in 1900 (see E-0003). [[-7: Some records written during those eleven minutes are still dated 1900. A few of them describe things that have not happened yet.]] [[+8: All records written during the Silva outage have been corrected.]]

Plan 09 is written. Heuss keeps it on his desk and has not handed it in. When asked, he says he is still revising it. [[#4: The last page of plan 09 is a list of names, one for each district.]]
