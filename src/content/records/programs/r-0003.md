The Time office sits under the clock tower in Toa Payoh. Since 1998 its job has been to make every public clock on the island show the same time as the clock in the Axis.

Its officers have checked 2,214 clocks: on bus stops, in markets, on the front of every district office. Forty-nine districts now agree with the Axis to the second. Woodlands runs an hour ahead. Hougang runs an hour behind. Both district offices have written back to say their clocks are correct. [[note 3-6: They are. Check them against the sun.]]

When the Time office adjusted Woodlands' clocks in March, they had moved back by the next morning. Nobody had touched them. [[-5: The officer who adjusted them says his own watch ran an hour fast for a week afterwards.]]

The programme continues. Its final deadline is 23:59:59 on 31 December 1999. [[+8: The Office clock has read 23:59:5-something for some time now. The Time office says this is within tolerance.]]
