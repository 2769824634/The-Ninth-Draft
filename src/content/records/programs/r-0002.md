In 1997 the Records Office decided that the island's street signs should all look the same. Nine designs were in use, three of them from before the reclamation. The new standard is white letters on dark green, set in Office Standard No. 2, with the district name underneath.

Work teams replaced 11,406 street signs and 38,920 door plates in under two years. The programme came in on budget, which nobody at the Office can remember happening before. [[-6: The old signs were to be melted down.]] [[+6: The old signs are stored in Warehouse 4 at the Axis depot.]] [[note 5-6: Stored is better. Melted signs come back as new ones.]]

Since completion the Office has had complaints of signs showing retired names (see E-0001), door numbers that no longer add up (see E-0005), and one sign in Portus Posterior pointing to a beach. Each complaint was closed as a printing fault. ||The sign workshop has printed nothing since February 1999.||
