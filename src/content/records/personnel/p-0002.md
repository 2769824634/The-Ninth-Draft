Heuss is twenty-one, the youngest technician at the Records Office. His job is "year-field remediation": making sure that after 31 December 1999, no record on the island writes the year as 1900.

He has handed in eight versions of the plan. Every one came back with the same reason: nothing wrong with the plan, but it cannot be approved. He has not handed in the ninth. When the Office asks, he says he is still revising it. [[note 8: Not yet. 31.12.99]]

In his spare time he takes photographs, always of the same street in Axis, at the same hour each week, from the same angle. He says that when the prints come back, the street name is sometimes not the one he saw when he pressed the shutter. ||He keeps the prints in a drawer, in date order. The label on the drawer reads 31.12.99.||

There is a rumour inside the Office that someone writes notes in the margins of the files, signed with a single name. The Office's formal position is that no such annotator exists.
