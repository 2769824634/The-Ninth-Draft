YosH is twenty-one and works for the Records Office on contract. On paper he only looks after the backup servers in the old machine room, twice a month.

The Office's own logs say otherwise. After several backflows he was the first one on the scene, and he took photographs. Asked why, he answered: it was on my way. [[-5: He was on the scene before the backflow was reported.]] [[note 3-5: Strike that. Nobody is early to a coincidence.]]

When his negatives are developed, some of the street signs in the frames do not match the street. The Office has confiscated part of them and issued receipts. Every receipt is dated the 9th. ||He has never handed in the reference numbers.||

They say he met Heuss at the coffee shop outside the Office. They are the same age and both live in Bukit Merah. Someone once saw Heuss's handwriting on the back of one of his negatives.
