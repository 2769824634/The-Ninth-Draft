Frank inspected the bridges around Ang Mo Kio. His job was to take a tape measure and confirm that each bridge was still yesterday's bridge.

His first report was already a problem. The north-bank bridge, he wrote, is about two centimetres longer on rainy days. [[-6: It is longer on every rainy day. It is never shorter.]] [[note 4-6: Thermal expansion. Write thermal expansion.]] The Records Office replied politely: thermal expansion, entirely normal, and please do not discuss the length of bridges outside of meetings.

After that he measured once more every week. The numbers did not change again, but he said the bridge was sometimes ||older than when he measured it||.

On the day he handed in his resignation, a colleague asked him why. He said, "I want to measure a bridge that doesn't get revised." The Records Office accepted it and filled in the reason: measurement error.
